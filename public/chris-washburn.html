01 · THE RISE

Before the headlines, there was a kid with a gift.

Chris Washburn came out of North Carolina with size, talent and expectations few players ever experience. NC State became the launchpad. In 1986, Golden State selected him third overall in the NBA Draft.

For a twenty-year-old, the dream arrived before he had learned how to carry it.

02 · THE FALL

The dream became the place he started losing himself.

Addiction consumed the promise of an NBA career. Money disappeared. Basketball disappeared. Stability disappeared. The descent eventually led through homelessness, incarceration and years of trying to survive the consequences of his own decisions.

Out of Bounds does not clean up that chapter. It tells it.

03 · THE TURN

At some point, surviving wasn't enough.

Chris reached the place where basketball could no longer save him, fame could no longer identify him and the past could no longer be rewritten. The only thing left to rebuild was the man.

“I had to reinvent my whole life.”

04 · THE REDEMPTION

The comeback had nothing to do with basketball.

Recovery gave Chris a second life. He rebuilt trust, created businesses, became a motivational speaker and began using the scars from his own journey to reach athletes, families and people fighting through their own valleys.

The player people once called a cautionary tale became someone capable of changing another person's direction.
