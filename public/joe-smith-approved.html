He knows what the dream looks like. He also knows the work it takes to reach it.

PLAYER → HALL OF FAMER → TRAINER

The game gave Joe a journey. Now he gives the journey back.

He competed at the highest level, earned recognition for what he accomplished, and came back to the gym with a different purpose: help young players see more in themselves.

One correction. One more rep. One coach close enough to notice the detail and patient enough to stay with it.

WORK → PROGRESS → CONFIDENCE

You can see the change before it ever shows up on a scoreboard.

Feet get sharper. Decisions get faster. The player asks for the ball again. The work becomes confidence, and confidence changes how a kid carries themselves on and off the court.
