# Money Behind It narration style

The host should sound like an intelligent, curious friend who happens to understand finance deeply.

Delivery: energetic but controlled, conversational, dryly funny, mildly sarcastic, curious before explanatory. Use natural emphasis and brief pauses before reveals. Jokes should feel tossed off, not performed. Avoid corporate cadence, consultant language, sales voice, classroom lecturing, or solemn documentary narration.

Editorial rule: story first, lesson second. Use curiosity gaps, memorable analogies, occasional punchlines, and a clear payoff without sacrificing factual accuracy.
