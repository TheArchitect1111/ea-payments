## Full description

```
Simplifi Orb helps you capture opportunities the moment they appear — from a link, a photo, or a note — and turns them into a clear daily Brief so nothing important slips away.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Sign in with a secure magic link (no password to remember)
• Capture URLs and photos of opportunities
• Review Today's Brief — what deserves attention now
• Track items in your Workspace / Inbox
• Enable push alerts for important updates

WHO IT'S FOR
Professionals and teams who want a simple capture → brief → follow-up loop without another complex CRM.

PRIVACY & AI
Simplifi may use AI to assist with analysis and summaries. Outputs are assistive — you stay in control. See our Privacy Policy, Terms, EULA, and AI Disclosure linked in the app and on our website.

SUPPORT
```
