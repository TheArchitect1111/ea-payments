# Amplifi Premium Design System

## Brand position

Amplifi is a calm, guided social media system. It reduces the weekly burden of research, creation, adaptation, approval, publishing, and learning without taking control away from the customer.

The experience should feel intelligent, editorial, human, and quietly capable. It should never feel like a generic AI dashboard or a collection of template cards.

## Identity

- Primary lockup: `public/amplifi/amplifi-logo-premium.png`
- Required endorsement: “by Efficiency Architects”
- Preserve clear space equal to the height of the capital A around the mark.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor individual letters, add shadows, or place the logo over busy photography.
- Use the full lockup for marketing and the symbol only for small product controls.

## Color

- Midnight Ink: `#0B1730`
- Guided Teal: `#09BDB9`
- Intelligence Blue: `#1D73DD`
- Momentum Violet: `#7550E7`
- Human Coral: `#FF735F`
- Warm Cream: `#F6F2E9`
- Paper: `#FFFDF9`
- Supporting Gray: `#5F6878`

Use midnight, cream, and paper as the dominant field colors. Teal, blue, violet, and coral show movement, state, and emphasis. Avoid rainbow distribution or decorative gradients with no information purpose.

## Typography

- Editorial display: Georgia or a licensed high-contrast editorial serif.
- Interface and supporting copy: Inter or a neutral modern sans serif.
- Headlines use short lines, generous breathing room, and restrained italics for the emotional turn.
- Body copy should remain between 55 and 75 characters per line.
- Eyebrows use uppercase sans serif, 8 to 11 pixels, strong tracking, and one accent color.

## Layout

- Prefer continuous editorial compositions over modular card grids.
- Alternate asymmetrical image, typography, interface, and color-field sections.
- Use visible paths, timelines, and relationships when explaining process.
- Cards are reserved for genuine product objects such as a draft, campaign, approval, or scheduled post.
- Do not place six equal feature cards in a grid to fill space.
- Use 96 to 128 pixels of vertical section spacing on desktop and 70 to 84 pixels on mobile.

## Photography

- Show believable leaders actively using the product.
- Favor natural expressions: relief, confidence, focus, and forward movement.
- Preserve faces, hands, devices, and important action.
- Use editorial lighting, warm neutrals, and restrained brand-color accents.
- Never reuse the same photograph on one page.
- Images must be rendered with explicit aspect ratio, object fit, focal point, and responsive sizes.

## Product views

- Product interfaces must demonstrate real work, not decorative dashboards.
- Use realistic campaign names, statuses, approvals, calendars, and channel actions.
- Mark sample information clearly when necessary.
- Desktop and mobile views must show the same underlying work from different contexts.
- Product copy must remain readable at the rendered size.

## Icons and diagrams

- Use thin, purposeful line icons with rounded joins.
- Icons explain actions, not decorate every heading.
- Workflow diagrams should feel continuous and directional.
- Avoid generic AI sparkles, brains, robots, floating nodes, meaningless charts, and stock infographics.

## Interaction and motion

- Motion should explain sequence or state change.
- Use subtle transitions between guided-demo stages.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Every interaction must have keyboard focus and an accessible name.

## Voice

- Guided, practical, calm, and specific.
- Begin with the customer’s work and pressure, then explain how Amplifi helps.
- Prefer “Amplifi prepares the work. You decide.” over exaggerated sales claims.
- Explain Social, Intelligence, Complete, and Smartchitecture in plain language.
- Do not use em dashes.

## Release checklist

- Correct logo and endorsement
- No broken, cropped, or repeated imagery
- No generic feature grids
- No clipped or overflowing text
- Full desktop, tablet, and mobile review
- Clear explanation of all three levels
- Clear Smartchitecture explanation
- Demonstrable desktop and mobile product experience
- Working calls to action and guided demo
- Strong contrast and keyboard accessibility
