# EA Marketing Brain

Status: installed chassis layer

## Purpose
The Marketing Brain is the strategy layer that runs before Smartchitecture, Amplifi, Foundry, page assembly, proposals or campaign production. It decides what should be made, why it should exist, what psychological job each section/asset performs, what proof is required, and how the visual story should carry the argument.

## Pipeline
INPUT -> MARKETING BRAIN -> SMARTCHITECTURE -> AMPLIFI / FOUNDRY / PAGE ASSEMBLY -> MARKETING QA -> EVA APPROVAL -> DEPLOY/PUBLISH

## Specialist roles
1. Audience Strategist: audience truth, jobs, language, tension.
2. Positioning Strategist: category, promise, differentiation, unique mechanism.
3. Conversion Architect: narrative order, objections, proof, CTA and progressive commitment.
4. Copy Director: hook, message hierarchy, specificity, voice and banned generic language.
5. Visual Story Director: emotional imagery, UI demonstrations, visual pacing, hierarchy and proof.
6. Marketing QA: hard gate for generic positioning, unsupported claims, weak CTA, repetitive layouts and mobile failure.
7. Learning Director: captures approvals, rejections, performance signals and brand knowledge for future runs.

## Open-source patterns adopted
We adopt architecture rather than another operational dependency/account:
- Marketing-and-Growth-Multi-Agent-System: research -> strategy -> content -> QA -> analytics; persistent brand memory and QA gates.
- BrandCrew: specialist agent roles, shared knowledge files, voice/boundary/quality memory and anti-slop filtering.
- MARA: persistent marketing memory, competitor/audience/strategy pipelines and human approval controls.
- Agentic Marketing Platform: strategy selection, grounded claims, governed learning and performance-driven optimization patterns.

EA remains the orchestrator and source of truth.

## Tarris / Player One media mode
Player One uses `mode: athlete`.

The Brain must never reduce an athlete to highlights. It manages five compounding brand pillars:
- ATHLETE: performance, training, development, competition.
- PERSON: identity, beliefs, interests, relationships, style and human moments.
- IMPACT: youth, camps, mentoring, community and causes.
- CULTURE: fashion, music, campus, lifestyle and authentic cultural relevance.
- ENTERPRISE: NIL concepts, partnerships, merchandise, appearances, camps, collaborations and owned ventures.

### TB3 Media decision loop
For each new moment or planned cycle:
1. What happened around Tarris?
2. Which brand pillar does it strengthen?
3. Why would someone beyond an Alabama basketball fan care?
4. Is there a larger story or recurring owned-IP series?
5. What content package should be built?
6. Is there a community, audience or commercial opportunity?
7. What evidence/assets are required?
8. What goes to Eva for approval?
9. What did audience response teach the Brain?

### Brand Room
The public/private partnership layer should make it easy for a brand to understand Tarris and imagine a legitimate activation. Campaign concepts must always be labeled as concepts when no partnership exists. Never fabricate endorsements, results, statistics or brand relationships.

## Required pre-production gate
Before a website, landing page, campaign, proposal or athlete story enters production, the Marketing Brain must answer:
- Who exactly is this for?
- What do they feel/want before they see us?
- What is the cost of the current state?
- What do we want them to believe afterward?
- Why us rather than an alternative?
- What is our unique mechanism?
- What proof makes that believable?
- What visual experience communicates it fastest?
- What should they do next?
- What should the system learn from the result?

If those answers are generic, production should stop and strategy should regenerate.
