## 10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact freedom@efficiencyarchitects.online. We may verify your request before responding.

### Account deletion (Simplifi Orb / Google Play)

You may delete your Simplifi Orb account and associated personal data:

1. **In the app:** Settings → Delete account (requires confirmation).
2. **On the web (no app install required):** https://efficiencyarchitects.online/legal/account-deletion
3. **By email:** freedom@efficiencyarchitects.online with subject “Account deletion request.”

Deletion revokes portal access, anonymizes contact fields on your client record, clears stored portal credentials, and removes push tokens for your account. Limited records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, or law.
