## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies (local storage, pixels) store small amounts of data on your device to operate and improve our Services.

## 2. Types we use

- **Strictly necessary** — authentication, security, load balancing
- **Preferences** — remembering settings
- **Analytics** — aggregated usage to improve experiences (where enabled)
- **Marketing** — only if a specific campaign feature is enabled and permitted

## 3. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through browser or device settings. Blocking necessary cookies may break login or checkout.
