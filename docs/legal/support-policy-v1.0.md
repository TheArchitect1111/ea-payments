## 1. Scope

This Support Policy describes how Ascension Systems provides support for platform products and related client experiences. Signed Statements of Work or subscription plans may provide enhanced support levels.

## 2. Channels

- Email: freedom@efficiencyarchitects.online  
- In-product Help / Contact your guide (portal clients)  
- Admin / ops channels for entitled enterprise clients

## 3. Business hours

Support is generally monitored **Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. U.S. Eastern Time**, excluding U.S. federal holidays, unless a signed agreement states otherwise.

## 4. Response targets (best effort)

| Priority | Examples | Target first response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Service fully unavailable for production clients | Within 1 business day |
| High | Major feature broken; workaround limited | Within 1–2 business days |
| Normal | Questions, how-to, non-blocking issues | Within 2–3 business days |

These are targets, not guarantees, unless an SLA is expressly included in a signed MSA/SOW.

## 5. Client responsibilities

- Provide accurate reproduction steps and account context
- Maintain secure credentials
- Designate a primary contact for paid engagements

## 6. Exclusions

Support does not include unpaid custom development, third-party product support, or issues caused by Client modifications outside supported configuration.
