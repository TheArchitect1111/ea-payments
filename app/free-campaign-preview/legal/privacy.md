# Privacy Policy - Amplifi
We collect: business name, type, goal, calendar availability (open chairs), posts generated, clicks/trials/revenue tracking via data/self-posts.json + income-report.json.
We DON'T collect: client customer data unless you connect calendar.
Storage: data/self-posts.json, data/income-report.json in your account.
No selling data. Stripe handles billing. Social posting via Ayrshare or native IG/FB API with your keys.
GDPR: Delete request -> delete data/self-posts.json + income-report.json.
Contact: support@amplifi
