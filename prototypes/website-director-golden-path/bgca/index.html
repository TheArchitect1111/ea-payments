After the bell

Millions of kids face empty afternoons.

When school ends, too many young people are alone — without a safe place, a caring adult, or a reason to believe someone is counting on them.

7.7M

kids and teens are alone and unsupervised after school — while nearly 25 million lack access to after-school programs.

Clubs get kids future ready.

Boys & Girls Clubs exist so every young person who walks through the door has a chance to thrive, lead, and shape the America that is coming.

From alone after school to known by name.

With a Club

Belonging. Mentors. A path forward.

Kids find peers, caring adults, and the confidence to graduate, volunteer, and stand up for what is right.
